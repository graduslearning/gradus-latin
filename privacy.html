Privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Gradus Latin: Vocab Master ("the app") is published by Christopher Eustace, trading as Gradus Learning, Queensland, Australia. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version

The app does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress stays on your own device.

What the app stores, and where

Your progress (medals, review dates, streak, badges, settings and which word lists you use) is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this information.

Purchases

Full Access is bought through Apple's App Store or Google Play. They handle the payment and tell the app only whether Full Access has been bought, so it can unlock the word lists. We never see your name, email address or payment details. Apple's and Google's own privacy policies cover the payment.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, your device schedules it locally. No information is sent anywhere, and you can turn it off at any time in the app's Settings or your device's settings.

Children

The app is designed for students, including children. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children. It contains no advertising and no links to social media. The only links that leave the app are to this website's support and privacy pages.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If the app ever starts to collect information, for example to offer accounts, this policy will say so before that happens.

Contact
