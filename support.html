Help and support

Need help, found a mistake in a word list, or have an idea? Email gradustheapp@gmail.com and we'll reply as soon as we can.

How do the medals work?

Every word climbs the same steps. Bronze is multiple choice: first pick the English for the Latin, then pick the Latin for the English. Silver is typing the English. Gold is typing the Latin, and a word at gold counts as learnt. Verbs reach platinum when all their principal parts are gold. Only the Tester earns medals; the games are for practice.

I bought Full Access but the lists are locked

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy it.

Can my family use Full Access?

Yes. On iPhone and iPad, Full Access is shared through Family Sharing. Each person taps Restore purchases on their own device.

Does my progress move to a new phone?

Progress is saved on the device you use, so it doesn't move between devices at the moment. Full Access does: use Restore purchases on the new device.

Reminders aren't appearing

Check that notifications are allowed for Gradus Latin in your device's settings, and that the daily reminder is on in the app's Settings.

Starting again

Settings › Reset everything clears all progress on your device. Full Access is not affected.
